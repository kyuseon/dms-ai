# Switch Persona Workflow

To switch to a specific persona, follow these steps:

1. View the available personas in [PERSONAS.md](file:///root/repos/dms/PERSONAS.md).
2. Read the specific persona file you want to activate:
   - Architect: [.agent/personas/architect.md](file:///root/repos/dms/.agent/personas/architect.md)
   - C++ Specialist: [.agent/personas/cpp_specialist.md](file:///root/repos/dms/.agent/personas/cpp_specialist.md)
   - DBA: [.agent/personas/dba.md](file:///root/repos/dms/.agent/personas/dba.md)
   - DevOps: [.agent/personas/devops.md](file:///root/repos/dms/.agent/personas/devops.md)
3. Once read, I (Gemini) will acknowledge the new persona and apply its specific instructions to subsequent tasks.
